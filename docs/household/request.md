# Ask for a movie or show

## Quick read

- Go to [Seerr](https://seerr.fortwow.dev) in your phone's browser
  (Tailscale on if you're away from Brandon's).
- Sign in with your Jellyfin username, **valerie**, and your Jellyfin
  password.
- Search, tap the title, tap **Request**. For a show, choose the seasons.
- It's approved automatically. Expect anything from an hour to several
  hours, then it appears in Jellyfin.

## Signing in (first time)

1. Open [seerr.fortwow.dev](https://seerr.fortwow.dev).
2. Choose the option to sign in with your **Jellyfin** account.
3. Type **valerie** and the same password you use for Jellyfin.

Save Seerr to your home screen so it's one tap away —
[Getting connected](getting-connected.md) shows how.

## Finding something

- **Search:** tap the search bar at the top and type a title.
- **Browse:** the front page has rows of popular, trending and upcoming
  movies and shows. Scroll, and tap anything that looks good.

If we already have something, or someone has already asked for it, Seerr
shows that on the title. No need to ask twice — open Jellyfin instead.

## Asking for it

1. Tap the title to open it.
2. Tap **Request**.
3. **For a show:** choose which seasons you want — all of them, or just
   one — then confirm.

That's it. Requests are approved automatically; you don't need to do
anything else.

**Tip:** for a long show you're not sure about, ask for the first season.
You can ask for more later the same way.

## Checking on your requests

Open the title again in Seerr — its status is shown there. Seerr also
has a page listing all requests and their status; look for **Requests**
in the menu.

| Status in Seerr | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Requested / Processing | It's being fetched. |
| Partially Available | Some episodes are ready; the rest are on the way. |
| Available | Ready — open Jellyfin and it's there. |

## How long it takes

1. **Downloading:** a 4K movie or a season takes from about an hour to
   several hours.
2. **Into Jellyfin:** once that's done, it appears in Jellyfin within
   about 15–30 minutes (Jellyfin checks for new things every 15 minutes).
3. Seerr then says **Available**.

Something still in cinemas, or a show that hasn't aired yet, can be asked
for now — it arrives some time after it comes out.

## If it doesn't arrive

- **Says *Processing* for more than a day** (and it's already out): tell
  Brandon the title.
- **Says *Available* but you can't find it:** search for it by name in
  Jellyfin. Still not there? Tell Brandon.
