# Listen to an audiobook

## Quick read

- Audiobooks are in **Audiobookshelf**, in the **Audiobooks** library —
  the same app as for reading.
- App: **Still: for Audiobookshelf** on iPhone, **Audiobookshelf** on
  Android — or open [Audiobookshelf](https://abs.fortwow.dev) in a
  browser.
- Open the **Audiobooks** library, tap a book, then **Play**.
- Audiobooks are new here and haven't been fully tried yet. If something
  doesn't work as described, tell Brandon.

## Getting started

If you've already set up the app for reading, skip to step 3.

1. Install **Still: for Audiobookshelf** (iPhone, App Store) or
   **Audiobookshelf** (Android, Play Store).
2. Enter the server address `https://abs.fortwow.dev`, then your username
   (**valerie**) and password.
3. Open the **Audiobooks** library.
4. Tap a book, then **Play**.

The library may still be empty — [find an audiobook with
Shelfmark](find-books.md) to add one.

## Handy controls

Look for these in the player. The icons differ a little between apps.

- **Speed:** the button showing a number like **1.0×**. Tap it to go
  faster or slower.
- **Sleep timer:** often a moon or clock icon. It stops playing after the
  time you choose — good for listening in bed.
- **Chapters:** a list button that lets you jump to a chapter.
- **Skip back or forward:** the circular arrow buttons, a few seconds at a
  time.

Audiobookshelf is made to remember your place and carry it between your
devices.

## Listening without internet

On the book's page, look for a **download** button (usually a downward
arrow). Tap it while you're on Wi-Fi. Once it's downloaded it should play
with no connection — handy for the car or a flight.

## In a web browser

On a computer, go to [abs.fortwow.dev](https://abs.fortwow.dev), sign in,
open the **Audiobooks** library and press play.
