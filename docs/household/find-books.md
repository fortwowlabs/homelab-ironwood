# Find a book or audiobook

Use **Shelfmark**: `https://shelfmark.fortwow.dev` (any browser — save it to
your home screen). There's no sign-in.

1. Search by title or author.
2. Choose whether you want the **ebook** or the **audiobook**.
3. Tap the one you want to get it.

## Picking an audiobook result

Choose one complete release — a single item, roughly a few hundred MB up to
about 1.5 GB, ideally with "m4b" in the name. Skip results numbered like
*[017/125]* — those are single pieces, not the whole book.

## What happens next

- **Ebooks** usually show up in your reading app within a few minutes — it
  can take up to about an hour if a big TV download is using the connection
  at the same time.
- **Audiobooks** are bigger and can take longer. They appear in
  Audiobookshelf when they're done.

Not there after a few hours? See [Something's not working](help.md).
