# Find a book or audiobook

## Quick read

- Go to [Shelfmark](https://shelfmark.fortwow.dev) in your browser. There's
  no sign-in.
- Search by title or author, choose **ebook** or **audiobook**, then pick
  a result to download it.
- Ebooks appear in the **Ebooks** library in
  [Audiobookshelf](https://abs.fortwow.dev), usually within a few minutes.
- Audiobooks are new here and may not work yet — tell Brandon if one
  doesn't arrive.

## Getting a book

1. Open [shelfmark.fortwow.dev](https://shelfmark.fortwow.dev) (Tailscale
   on if you're away from Brandon's).
2. Search by the book's title or the author's name.
3. Choose whether you want the **ebook** or the **audiobook**.
4. Pick a result to download it.

Can't find it? Try just the author's surname, or fewer words of the
title. If a download fails, try a different result.

Save Shelfmark to your home screen so it's one tap away —
[Getting connected](getting-connected.md) shows how.

## Picking an audiobook result

Choose one complete release:

- a single item, roughly a few hundred MB up to about 1.5 GB;
- ideally with "m4b" in the name.

Skip results numbered like *[017/125]* — those are single pieces, not the
whole book.

## Where it shows up

- **Ebooks** go to the **Ebooks** library in Audiobookshelf — see
  [Read a book](read.md). Usually within a few minutes; up to about an
  hour if a big TV download is being processed at the same time.
- **Audiobooks** go to the **Audiobooks** library — see
  [Listen to an audiobook](listen.md). They're bigger and can take longer.

## If it doesn't show up

1. In Audiobookshelf, refresh the library: pull down on the list, or
   close and reopen the app.
2. Give an ebook up to an hour.
3. Still missing? Tell Brandon the title, and whether it was the ebook or
   the audiobook.
