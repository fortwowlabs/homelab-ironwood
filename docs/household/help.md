# Something's not working

Try the first check for your problem. If that doesn't fix it, tell Brandon
what you were doing and what you saw.

| Problem | First check |
|---|---|
| Nothing loads, away from home | Turn on **Tailscale** ([how](getting-connected.md)). Still nothing with Tailscale on? Tell Brandon — the problem may be on his end. |
| Nothing loads, at home | Check you're on the home Wi-Fi, not mobile data. |
| A video buffers a lot, away from home | Most of what we have is in 4K, which is heavy on mobile data. Try a lower quality in the player's settings, or wait for Wi-Fi. |
| A show stutters badly only with subtitles on | Turn subtitles off and tell Brandon. |
| A request is stuck on *Processing* | Give it a day — 4K can take several hours on its own. After that, tell Brandon the title. |
| A book never showed up | Ebooks: pull down to refresh the library, and allow up to an hour. Audiobooks can take longer — after a day, tell Brandon. |
| Forgot a password | Ask Brandon — he can reset it. |
