# Watch movies and TV

## Quick read

- Open the **Jellyfin** app (on iPhone it's called **Swiftfin**), or go
  to [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.fortwow.dev) in a browser.
- Away from Brandon's? Turn **Tailscale** on first — on the TV at your
  place too.
- Pick **Movies** or **Shows**, choose something, press **Play**. It
  remembers where you stopped, on every device.
- If a show stutters with subtitles on, turn subtitles off and tell
  Brandon.

## Signing in the first time

You do this once on each device.

- **Server address:** `https://jellyfin.fortwow.dev` — after that the
  app shows it by name, **Fort Wow**.
- **Username:** **valerie**. Type it in — the sign-in screen won't list
  names to tap.
- Then the password Brandon gave you.

## On the TV at Brandon's (Google TV Streamer)

1. On the TV's home screen, open **Jellyfin**.
2. First time only: enter the server address, then type your username and
   password.
3. Pick **Movies** or **Shows**, choose something, press **Play**.

While it's playing, press the middle (OK) button on the remote to bring
up the controls. Press **Back** to stop.

## On the TV at your place

1. Check **Tailscale** on the TV is on —
   [how](getting-connected.md).
2. Open **Jellyfin**. If it isn't installed, search the TV's app store
   for **Jellyfin for Android TV** and install it.
3. First time only: sign in the same way as above.

If Jellyfin won't load even with Tailscale on, tell Brandon — the problem
may be on his end.

## On iPhone or iPad (Swiftfin)

1. Install **Swiftfin** from the App Store.
2. Open it and choose to connect to a server. Enter the server address.
3. When **Fort Wow** appears, sign in: type your username and password.
4. Pick something and tap **Play**.

## On Android (Jellyfin)

1. Install **Jellyfin** from the Play Store.
2. Enter the server address.
3. Type your username and password to sign in.
4. Pick something and tap **Play**.

## In a web browser

On any computer or phone, go to
[jellyfin.fortwow.dev](https://jellyfin.fortwow.dev) and sign in. Handy
on a laptop.

## Finding your way around

- **Carry on where you left off:** the home screen has a row of things
  you've started, and one with the next episode of shows you're
  following. (They're usually called something like **Continue
  Watching** and **Next Up**.)
- **Switch devices:** stop on one device, open the same title on another,
  and it offers to pick up where you stopped.
- **Search:** tap the magnifying glass.
- **Mark as watched:** open a movie or episode and look for a tick (✓)
  button. Tap it again to undo.
- **Favourites:** tap the heart on anything you like, so it's easy to
  find again.

## Subtitles and audio language

1. While it's playing, tap the screen (or press OK on the remote) to show
   the controls.
2. Look for the **subtitles** button — often a speech bubble or a "CC"
   icon. Pick a language, or **Off**.
3. To change the spoken language, look for the **audio** button next to
   it. Some apps keep both inside a settings (gear) menu instead.

**If a show stutters with subtitles on, turn subtitles off and tell
Brandon.** It's a known problem with some 4K shows.

## Away from Brandon's, and offline

Most of what we have is in 4K. It plays great on the TVs, but on a phone
away from Brandon's it may buffer — see
[Something's not working](help.md).

Some phone apps can **download** a movie or episode to watch with no
connection (a plane, the car). If yours can, there's a download button
(usually a downward arrow) on the movie or episode. Tap it while you're on
Wi-Fi. 4K films are big, so check your phone has space. No download
button? Ask Brandon.
