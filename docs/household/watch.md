# Watch movies and TV

Everything we have is in **Jellyfin** — it shows up listed as **Fort Wow**.
Server address: `https://jellyfin.fortwow.dev`

## On the TV (Google TV Streamer, at Brandon's)

1. On the home screen, open **Jellyfin**. (First time: install it from
   **Apps → Search → Jellyfin**.)
2. First time only: enter the server address above, then type your username
   and password — the sign-in screen won't list names to tap.
3. Pick **Movies** or **Shows**, choose something, press **Play**.

Stopping partway is fine — it remembers where you were, on every device.

## On the TV at your place

That TV also needs Tailscale turned on before Jellyfin will load — check
it's connected first. After that, sign in the same way as above.

## On iPhone or iPad

1. Install **Swiftfin** from the App Store.
2. Tap **Connect to Server**, enter the server address, then type your
   username and password to sign in.
3. Pick something and tap **Play**.

## On Android

1. Install **Jellyfin** from the Play Store.
2. Enter the server address, then type your username and password to sign
   in.
3. Pick something and tap **Play**.

## Subtitles

While it's playing, open the player menu and choose **Subtitles**.

## Watching without internet (plane, car)

Most of what we have is in 4K, which can buffer over mobile data. On your
phone, open the movie or episode and tap **Download** while you're still on
Wi-Fi — downloads play with no connection at all.
