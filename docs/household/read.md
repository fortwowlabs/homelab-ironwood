# Read a book

## Quick read

- Your ebooks are in **Audiobookshelf**, in the **Ebooks** library.
- App: **Still: for Audiobookshelf** on iPhone, **Audiobookshelf** on
  Android — or open [Audiobookshelf](https://abs.fortwow.dev) in a
  browser.
- Server address `https://abs.fortwow.dev`, username **valerie**.
- Open the **Ebooks** library, tap a book, tap **Read**.
- Want something new? [Find a book with Shelfmark](find-books.md).

## iPhone or iPad

1. Install **Still: for Audiobookshelf** from the App Store.
2. Enter the server address `https://abs.fortwow.dev`, then your username
   and password.
3. Open the **Ebooks** library, tap a book, tap **Read**.

## Android

1. Install **Audiobookshelf** from the Play Store.
2. Enter the server address `https://abs.fortwow.dev`, then your username
   and password.
3. Switch to the **Ebooks** library, tap a book, tap **Read**.

## In a web browser

On a computer or tablet, go to
[abs.fortwow.dev](https://abs.fortwow.dev), sign in, and open the
**Ebooks** library. Books open right in the browser.

## While you're reading

- **Turn the page:** swipe, or tap near the edge of the page.
- **Show the controls:** tap the middle of the page.
- **Bigger text, dark background:** look in the reader's settings (often
  a gear or an "Aa" button) for text size and a light or dark theme.

## Your place is saved

Audiobookshelf remembers where you stopped. Start on your phone, carry on
on your tablet or in the browser.

## If you read a lot: Readest

Readest is a separate reading app with more ways to adjust fonts and page
turning. It uses your separate book-library account (ask Brandon if you
don't have one). Your username there is **valerie** too, but the password
is the book-library one, not your Jellyfin one.

1. Install **Readest** (App Store or Play Store).
2. Find the option to add an online catalog (it may be called **OPDS**).
   Enter the address `https://calibre-web.fortwow.dev/opds` and your
   book-library username and password.
3. Browse the catalog, and tap a book to open it.

To check your book-library account works, sign in at
[calibre-web.fortwow.dev](https://calibre-web.fortwow.dev) in a browser.

We haven't tested yet whether Readest keeps your place between devices —
for now, read each book on one device.

Readest and Audiobookshelf don't share your place with each other. Pick
one app for each book.
