# Getting connected

## At home

On the home Wi-Fi everything just works. Nothing to set up.

## Away from home

Your phone needs **Tailscale** turned on. It's a free app that makes your
phone act as if it were at home.

### iPhone

1. Install **Tailscale** from the App Store.
2. Open it and sign in with the account Brandon invited you with.
3. Tap the switch so it says **Connected**.

### Android

1. Install **Tailscale** from the Play Store.
2. Open it and sign in with the account Brandon invited you with.
3. Tap **Connect**.

### The TV at your place

It has Tailscale installed too. Before opening Jellyfin on it, check that
Tailscale on that TV is connected — if Jellyfin won't load there, that's the
first thing to check.

Leave Tailscale on when you're out. If a page won't load away from home,
check Tailscale first.
