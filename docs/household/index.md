# Home guide

## Quick read

- **First time?** Start with [Getting connected](getting-connected.md).
- **Away from Brandon's?** Turn **Tailscale** on first, or nothing here
  will load — this guide included.
- **Watch** in [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.fortwow.dev). **Ask for** a
  movie or show in [Seerr](https://seerr.fortwow.dev).
- **Get a book** in [Shelfmark](https://shelfmark.fortwow.dev), then
  **read or listen** in [Audiobookshelf](https://abs.fortwow.dev).
- **Stuck?** See [Something's not working](help.md), or ask Brandon.

## What do you want to do?

Tap **How** for step-by-step help, or **Open** to go straight there.

| I want to… | How | Open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watch a movie or TV show | [Watch](watch.md) | [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.fortwow.dev) |
| Get a movie or show we don't have | [Ask for one](request.md) | [Seerr](https://seerr.fortwow.dev) |
| Get an ebook or audiobook | [Find a book](find-books.md) | [Shelfmark](https://shelfmark.fortwow.dev) |
| Read a book | [Read](read.md) | [Audiobookshelf](https://abs.fortwow.dev) |
| Listen to an audiobook | [Listen](listen.md) | [Audiobookshelf](https://abs.fortwow.dev) |

The **Open** links work in any browser. For watching, reading and
listening, the phone and TV apps are nicer — each **How** page says which
app to use.

## First time? Do these in order

1. [Getting connected](getting-connected.md) — turn on Tailscale and
   install the apps.
2. [Watch something](watch.md) — sign in to Jellyfin once on each device.
3. [Ask for something](request.md) — the next time we don't have what you
   want.
